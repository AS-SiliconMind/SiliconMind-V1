Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 31, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about how visitors use our website. This helps us understand how our site is being used and improve the user experience.

Data Collected by Google Analytics

Google Analytics collects the following types of information:

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and device type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, and navigation paths
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location (country, city) based on IP address
  • Referral Information: How you arrived at our website (e.g., search engine, direct link, social media)
  • Session Information: Date and time of visits, session duration, and bounce rate

How We Use This Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Understand visitor demographics and interests
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve website content and user experience
  • Monitor website performance and identify technical issues

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help track your interactions with our website. These cookies do not contain personally identifiable information.

The cookies used by Google Analytics include:

  • _ga: Used to distinguish users (expires after 2 years)
  • _ga_<ID>: Used to persist session state (expires after 2 years)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. The data collected by Google Analytics is processed by Google in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by:

  • Installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Adjusting your browser settings to block cookies
  • Using browser extensions that block tracking scripts

Data Retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the project's GitHub repository.
